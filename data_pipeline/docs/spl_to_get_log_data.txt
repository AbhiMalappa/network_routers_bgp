``` trim added 2026-09-30: substr cuts at a fixed character position and can
land right after a space, leaving a trailing space substr itself never
removes (it's a single space, not a "run" the \s+ replace above would
touch). This exact artifact broke exact-match message_text lookups during
the triage this fix came from -- 'neighbor ' and 'dsp slot changed state '
both carried a hidden trailing space that made them silently fail to match
anything, including themselves when re-typed by hand. Trimming after the
cut, not before, is the only position that actually guards against it. ```
